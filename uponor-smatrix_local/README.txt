Uponor Smatrix Local adds each room of your Uponor Smatrix Pulse floor heating system to Homey as a sensor. Every room shows its temperature, and rooms whose thermostat has a humidity sensor show humidity too. Rooms keep the names you gave them in the Uponor system.
The app talks directly to the Uponor R-208 communication module on your local network, so it needs no Uponor account and no internet connection. When adding a device, enter the IP address of the R-208 and pick the rooms you want. Give the R-208 a fixed IP address in your router so Homey can always find it.
